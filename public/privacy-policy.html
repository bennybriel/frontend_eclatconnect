ECLAT CONNECT Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Official Production Document

Summary: ECLAT CONNECT (https://eclatconnect.com) is committed to protecting the privacy of students, residents, artisans, and business owners in Ogbomoso and the LAUTECH community. We collect only the information necessary to facilitate direct connections and verify accounts. We never sell your personal information to third parties.

1. Introduction

ECLAT CONNECT is a mobile-first local discovery and direct-connection platform connecting customers with businesses, campus products, skilled professionals, and services around LAUTECH and Ogbomoso, Oyo State, Nigeria.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Name, email address, password hash, role classification, and verified Nigerian telephone/WhatsApp phone number.
  • Vendor & Business Details: Trade name, service category, campus/town hub location (Under G, Adenike, Aroje, General, etc.), landmark address, photos, and public contact numbers.
  • Interaction Data: Direct call & WhatsApp enquiry clicks, customer service requests, product listings, and ratings/reviews.
  • Technical Data: IP address, device telemetry, browser type, and timestamp logs used for fraud prevention and performance security.

3. How We Use Information

  • To display verified public profiles, services, and products to potential buyers and clients.
  • To enable direct phone and WhatsApp communication between buyers and local providers without intermediary fees.
  • To deliver transactional notifications, OTP security codes, account updates, and administrative announcements via in-app alerts and WhatsApp.
  • To verify provider authenticity, review quality, and mitigate fraudulent listings.

4. WhatsApp Cloud API & Third-Party Services

We use Meta’s official WhatsApp Cloud API for customer messaging and authorized broadcasts, SmartSMSSolutions for transactional SMS verification, and Zoho CPAAS for transactional emails. All third-party providers comply with applicable data protection requirements and are restricted from unauthorized use.

5. Data Security & Storage

All data transmitted to ECLAT CONNECT is encrypted with TLS 1.3 (HTTPS). Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed. Database access is strictly secured via role-based access control.

6. Your Rights

In accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your account and personal listings, or opt out of promotional broadcasts.
